# Domain lexicon (referenced by plainpolish)

Source: 43 research papers published in 2021 or earlier, about 330,000 words. ES&T 12, Water Research 10 (including Water Research X), Nature journals 4, Environment International 6, STOTEN 7, Environmental Pollution 4. Papers from 2022 onwards were excluded because they may contain AI-polished text.

## 1. Domain terms (keep as written, do not replace with synonyms)

antibiotic resistance, resistance genes (ARGs), antibiotic-resistant bacteria (ARB), selection pressure, minimal selective concentration (MSC), predicted no-effect concentration (PNEC), conjugative plasmids, plasmid loss, plasmid transfer, horizontal gene transfer (HGT), fitness cost, activated sludge, municipal wastewater, wastewater treatment plant (WWTP), effluent / influent, sewage, sludge, faecal / fecal pollution, faecal indicator bacteria (FIB), E. coli, ESBL E. coli, CFU, oocyst, risk assessment, QMRA, dose–response, exposure, recreational water, water quality, removal (efficiency), log reduction, survival / decay / inactivation, first-order decay, dilution, point source / diffuse source, hotspot, river basin, Monte Carlo, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty, validation, Spearman, RMSE.

Follow the user's spelling (British or American: faecal/fecal, modelling/modeling) and do not unify it.

## 2. Common verbs and fixed phrases

- Reporting results: show / showed / shown, found, observed (most common, 185 uses), indicated, suggested, demonstrated (24 uses, mostly where an experiment directly supports the result), revealed, identified
- Methods: applied, compared, measured, collected, performed, conducted, calculated, estimated, simulated, predicted, obtained, derived, evaluated, assessed, considered, included, excluded, assumed, adopted, accounted for
- Models: is calculated as, is represented by, is described by, was parameterized, was set to, was fitted, were taken from
- Comparison and agreement: in line with, consistent with, similar to, comparable to, in contrast to, differs from
- Proportions and magnitudes: orders of magnitude, log10 higher/lower, an X-fold increase, X-fold higher, within / outside the range of, ranged from X to Y
- Causes: due to, is attributed to, is likely caused by, can be explained by, as a result of, results from
- Applications and value: provides insight into, can guide, can inform, serve as a basis for, opens opportunities for

## 3. Limitations of this lexicon

- The sample is 43 papers, of which only 4 are Nature journals and 4 are Environmental Pollution, so differences between journals are only indicative.
- The STOTEN and Environmental Pollution samples include reviews and papers by non-native authors; their language is less consistent than ES&T, Water Research or Nature, so use them only as a vocabulary and terminology reference.
- The lexicon describes how people in this field write; the user's feedback takes precedence.
